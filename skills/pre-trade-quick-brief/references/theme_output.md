## Theme/Sector Ranking Output

Use this mode when the user asks "which names is the market pushing", "what is being traded in this theme", or compares many tickers.

### 0. Information Coverage

One line with coverage and missing data.

### 1. 2-Minute Sector Conclusion

Five bullets or fewer:

- The most pushed name and top 3-5 ranked names.
- Why the top name wins versus peers.
- Whether each top name is "high market heat" or "high fundamental delivery".
- The 1-2 sector variables that matter most.
- The biggest sector-level kill point.

### 2. Ranking Table

| Rank | Company / ticker | Exchange | Value-chain position | Market-push level | Fundamental delivery | Recommendation reason | Catalyst | Main risk | Source/date |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use value-chain language, for example: CDU, cold plate, manifold, quick connector, coolant, immersion, server rack integration, power/thermal total solution, component supplier, or OEM/ODM.

### 3. Why This Name, Not The Others

For the top 3-5 names, write:

- What it sells.
- Who it sells to.
- How it gets paid.
- Revenue share/order evidence for the narrative business.
- Why the market is discussing it now.
- Why it ranks above/below peers.

### 4. Priced-In Check

For each top name, state what the stock already appears to price in:

- Order scale.
- Margin trajectory.
- Customer penetration.
- Revenue/profit contribution timing.
- Valuation multiple versus history/peers, when available.

### 5. Sector Long Logic Top 3

Each point must have numbers or verifiable signals.

### 6. Sector Short Logic Top 3

Must include:

- Valuation has priced in aggressive assumptions.
- Orders or customer qualification may not convert.
- Competition, price cuts, or gross-margin pressure.

### 7. Verification Points And Kill Criteria

Give 1-2 quarter checks for the sector and the top names:

- Order announcements or backlog conversion.
- Customer qualification / sample / mass-production milestones.
- Revenue share of the narrative business.
- Gross margin trend.
- Capex, capacity, delivery, or supply-chain bottlenecks.
